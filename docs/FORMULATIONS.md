# Web Fluid Master Catalog: Working & Theorized Versions

This catalog documents the exact preparation protocols, confirmed working versions, and planned engineering iterations of the sprayable web fluid.

---

## 🏷️ Versioning Standard
- **Major Versions (1.0, 2.0, 3.0...):** Finalized milestone systems or major completed engineering phases.
- **Minor Versions (1.1, 1.2, 1.3...):** Incremental improvements, material changes, ratio optimizations, or solvent adjustments.
- **Status Tags:**
  - `[WORKING]` — Physically prepared, sprayed, and confirmed to form airborne strands.
  - `[THEORIZED]` — Formulated by research, ready for bench validation.

---

## 📋 Catalog of Versions

### `web-fluid-1.0` [WORKING]
*The Glassy Polymer Baseline (Proof-of-Concept)*

- **Status:** Validated / Baseline
- **Polymer Base:** Polystyrene (Thermocol / Expanded Polystyrene)
- **Solvent:** Thinner SP-85
- **Hardware:** Plastic spray bottle atomizer
- **Preparation Protocol (The Two-Step Method):**
  1. **Step 1 — Base Concentrate:** Mix Thinner SP-85 and thermocol pieces in a mixing container, stirring until the thermocol fully dissolves and the fluid becomes slightly thicker than water (approximately $20\%\text{–}30\%$ more viscous than water).
  2. **Step 2 — Atomizer Dilution:** Transfer the base concentrate into the plastic spray bottle, then add pure Thinner SP-85 in a **$40:60$ ratio** ($40\%$ base concentrate, $60\%$ fresh thinner). Swirl gently to blend.
- **Observed Behavior:**
  - Sprays into multi-strand airborne web filaments.
  - **Limitation:** Extremely brittle / crumbly (Rating D1). Strands shatter into powdery dust upon touch because polystyrene is a glassy plastic at room temperature ($T_g \approx 100^\circ\text{C}$).

---

### `web-fluid-1.2` [WORKING]
*The Synthetic Rubber Breakthrough*

- **Status:** Active Working Prototype
- **Polymer Base:** Fevibond (Synthetic Polychloroprene / Neoprene Rubber Contact Adhesive)
- **Solvent:** Thinner SP-85
- **Hardware:** Plastic spray bottle atomizer
- **Preparation Protocol (The Two-Step Method):**
  1. **Step 1 — Base Concentrate:** Mix Thinner SP-85 and Fevibond adhesive in a container, stirring until fully dissolved into a uniform mixture approximately $20\%\text{–}30\%$ more viscous than water.
  2. **Step 2 — Atomizer Dilution:** Add the Fevibond base concentrate into the plastic spray bottle, followed by Thinner SP-85 in a **$40:60$ ratio** ($40\%$ base concentrate, $60\%$ fresh thinner). Shake or swirl to homogenize.
- **Observed Behavior:**
  - Sprays into cohesive, airborne web-like strands similar in appearance to 1.0.
  - **Major Improvement:** **Non-brittle, flexible, and ductile**. The strands do not crumble into dust when handled and exhibit clear potential for tensile strength.
  - **Scientific Mechanism:** Fevibond’s synthetic neoprene rubber has a glass transition temperature far below room temperature ($T_g \approx -40^\circ\text{C}$), allowing the polymer chains to flex and stretch instead of shattering.

### `web-fluid-1.3` [THEORIZED]
*Concentrate-to-Thinner Ratio Tuning (Thicker Strands)*

- **Status:** Ready for Bench Testing
- **Objective:** Test if increasing the base concentrate ratio creates thicker, tougher strands without clogging the spray nozzle.
- **Polymer Base:** Fevibond Synthetic Rubber
- **Solvent:** Thinner SP-85
- **Proposed Dilution Tests:**
  - **Ratio A (Lean - 30:70):** $30\%$ Base Fluid : $70\%$ Thinner SP-85 (checks lower limit; expect fine cobwebs, high spray velocity).
  - **Ratio B (Current Baseline - 40:60):** The confirmed working `web-fluid-1.2` standard.
  - **Ratio C (Rich - 50:50):** $50\%$ Base Fluid : $50\%$ Thinner SP-85 (tests for thicker, rope-like web strands).
  - **Ratio D (Heavy - 60:40):** $60\%$ Base Fluid : $40\%$ Thinner SP-85 (tests maximum viscosity limit before nozzle clogging).

---

### `web-fluid-1.4` [THEORIZED]
*Rubber-Polystyrene Hybrid (High-Modulus Alloy)*

- **Status:** Research Concept
- **Objective:** Blend a small amount of thermocol base fluid into the Fevibond base fluid (e.g., $85\%$ Fevibond base + $15\%$ Thermocol base, diluted $40:60$ with SP-85).
- **Goal:** Use polystyrene as a rigid skeletal backbone to give the rubber web higher tensile stiffness without making it brittle.

### `web-fluid-2.0` [THEORIZED]
*The First Finalized High-Strength System*

- **Status:** Reserved for Next Major Milestone
- **Criteria for Version 2.0:** A stable, optimized web fluid that consistently bridges a $30\text{ cm}$ gap, dries tack-free in $<2$ seconds, and holds a measurable weight (e.g., small tool, battery, or keys) without breaking.
