# Experiment Log: web-fluid-1.0 (Polystyrene / Thermocol + SP-85 Thinner)

---

## 1. Formulation & System Parameters
- **Formulation Name:** `web-fluid-1.0`
- **Polymer Base:** Expanded Polystyrene (Thermocol / EPS packaging foam)
- **Solvent:** Thinner SP-85
- **Hardware:** Standard plastic spray bottle atomizer
- **Preparation Method (Two-Step Dilution):**
  1. **Base Fluid:** Dissolved thermocol into Thinner SP-85 until the solution became approximately $20\%\text{–}30\%$ more viscous than water.
  2. **Atomizer Charge:** Added the base fluid into the plastic spray bottle and diluted with fresh Thinner SP-85 in a **$40:60$ ratio** ($40\%$ base fluid, $60\%$ thinner).

---

## 2. Experimental Observations & Results
- **Dissolution:** Thermocol dissolved rapidly into the SP-85 thinner, collapsing foam gas pockets into a clear solution.
- **Spray & Web Formation:**
  - Ejected through the plastic atomizer and successfully formed fine web-like filaments in mid-air.
  - Proved that the physical mechanism (flash-evaporative solution blow spinning) works with accessible hardware and SP-85 thinner.
- **Mechanical Properties:**
  - **Severe Brittleness:** Strands crumbled into powdery foam dust upon touch.
  - **Ductility Rating:** D1 (Glassy / Crumbly).
  - **Load Capacity:** Nil. Zero tensile utility due to polystyrene's glassy nature ($T_g \approx 100^\circ\text{C}$).

---

## 3. Key Takeaway & Transition to 1.2
- Successfully proved atomization into fibers.
- Prompted the transition from rigid polystyrene to flexible synthetic rubber (Fevibond), leading directly to `web-fluid-1.2`.
