# Formulation & Test Plan: web-fluid-1.3

---

## 🎯 Version Goal: `web-fluid-1.3`
`web-fluid-1.2` proved that synthetic rubber eliminates brittleness. 

**`web-fluid-1.3` is designed to multiply the strand thickness, structural integrity, and tensile breaking strength** of the web fluid system.

---

## 🧪 Formulation Specifications (`web-fluid-1.3`)

### 1. Base Materials
- **Polymer Base:** Fevibond Synthetic Neoprene Rubber Adhesive
- **Solvent:** Thinner SP-85
- **Hardware:** Spray bottle atomizer with stream-tuned nozzle

### 2. Preparation Method (High-Density Two-Step Protocol)

#### Step 1: Concentrated Base Preparation
- Dissolve Fevibond into Thinner SP-85 until it reaches a **high-viscosity concentrate** (approximately $40\%\text{–}50\%$ thicker than water, visibly syrup-like).

#### Step 2: Formulation Ratios to Test
Instead of the thin $40:60$ ratio used in `1.2`, `1.3` tests two high-density ratios:

- **Formula 1.3-A (Medium-Dense):**
  - $50\%$ Concentrated Base Fluid
  - $50\%$ Fresh Thinner SP-85
  - *Goal:* Moderate increase in strand diameter and tensile resistance.

- **Formula 1.3-B (High-Dense / Target Maximum):**
  - $60\%$ Concentrated Base Fluid
  - $40\%$ Fresh Thinner SP-85
  - *Goal:* Maximum rubber mass per ejection $\rightarrow$ thick, ropy strands capable of supporting weight.

---

## 🔬 Testing & Evaluation Protocol for `1.3`

1. **Strand Diameter Check:** Compare 1.3-A and 1.3-B strands against `1.2` baseline under bright light.
2. **Gap Bridge Test:** Spray across a $15\text{ cm}$ fixed gap.
3. **Hand-Pull Tensile Test:** Measure pull resistance before parting.
4. **Nozzle Check:** Verify if the spray bottle pumps smoothly without tip-skinning.

---

## 📈 Expected Milestone for `1.3`
If `web-fluid-1.3` successfully produces thick, load-bearing rubber strands that do not snap easily, it completes the density optimization phase and clears the path toward **Version 2.0**.
