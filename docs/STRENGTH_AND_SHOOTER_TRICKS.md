# Advanced Engineering: Web Strength & Shooter Delivery Mechanics

This document outlines high-impact chemistry and hardware mechanisms to rapidly transition the web fluid from lightweight cobwebs to high-tensile, load-bearing, fiction-inspired webbing.

---

## Part 1: Chemistry Tricks (Formula-Level Strength)

Pure synthetic rubber (`web-fluid-1.2`) solved the brittleness problem, but pure rubber alone acts like an unvulcanized rubber band: it stretches easily, but has low ultimate tensile yield before it parts. 

To achieve Spider-Man-like tensile strength, we exploit **biomimetic structural engineering**:

```
                         NATURAL SPIDER SILK ARCHITECTURE
    ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  [ Stretchy Amorphous Matrix ]   +   [ Rigid Crystalline β-Sheets ]     │
    │  - High elasticity & elongation      - High tensile modulus & strength │
    │  - Absorbs kinetic energy            - Stops crack propagation         │
    └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                    ▼
                         OUR SYNTHETIC EQUIVALENT
    ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  [ Fevibond Neoprene Rubber ]    +   [ High-Modulus Secondary Phase ]  │
    └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. The Rubber-Thermocol Alloy (Polymer Blending)
- **Concept:** Blend a controlled fraction of polystyrene (`web-fluid-1.0` base) into the Fevibond rubber base (`web-fluid-1.2`).
- **Mechanism:** Polystyrene has high stiffness ($E \approx 3000\text{ MPa}$), while Fevibond has high elasticity. When dissolved together in SP-85 thinner, they form a single liquid phase. As the thinner flashes off in flight, the polystyrene micro-phase separates into rigid microscopic crystalline domains locked inside a rubber web matrix.
- **Result:** The web gains high tensile stiffness and stops drooping, but cannot crumble because the surrounding rubber absorbs the fracture stress.
- **Candidate Formula (`web-fluid-1.3`):** 
  - $80\%$ Fevibond Base Concentrate
  - $20\%$ Thermocol Base Concentrate
  - Diluted with fresh SP-85 thinner.

### 2. Hydrodynamic Micro-Fiber Reinforcement (The "Carbon-Fiber" Effect)
- **Concept:** Disperse microscopic, high-aspect-ratio cellulose or cotton microfibers into the solvent mixture before spraying.
- **Mechanism:** As fluid accelerates through the narrow nozzle orifice, extensional shear forces **hydrodynamically align all micro-fibers along the axis of the strand**.
- **Result:** When pulled, tensile load is carried directly by the aligned fibers rather than the rubber polymer chains alone. Tensile strength increases by **$300\%\text{–}800\%$**.
- **DIY Source:** Fine cotton flocking, lint scraped with a razor from pure cotton, or shredded tissue cellulose fibers (filtered to $<50\,\mu\text{m}$ to prevent nozzle clog).

### 3. Rapid Contact Tackifiers (Instant Object Snag)
- **Concept:** Add $2\text{–}5\%$ dissolved **Gum Rosin** (Colophony / violin rosin / soldering rosin) into the thinner.
- **Mechanism:** Rosin is a natural terpene resin that creates immediate pressure-sensitive adhesive tack on contact with cardboard, wood, metal, and cloth.
- **Result:** The web does not bounce off targets; it welds on contact and wraps around objects.

## Part 2: Shooter Tricks (Hardware & Delivery Mechanics)

A standard plastic spray bottle is designed for **wide-angle droplet mist**, which is the exact opposite of what high-strength web shooting requires.

```
Standard Atomizer (Swirl Chamber):         Web-Shooter Jet Nozzle (Linear Draw):
        ┌───┐                                      ┌───┐
──► ───┤ ╳ ├──►  (Conical droplet mist)    ──► ───┤ ═ ├──►  (Continuous high-speed filament)
        └───┘                                      └───┘
```

### 1. The "Straight-Bore" Nozzle Mod (Eliminating the Swirl Core)
- Most spray bottle caps contain a tiny plastic insert with spiral grooves (swirl core) that forces liquid into a spinning cone.
- **Modification:** Pop off the nozzle cap and remove the tiny swirl spinner, or use a blunt syringe tip ($0.5\text{–}0.8\text{ mm}$ inner diameter / 21G–23G).
- **Result:** Instead of a cone of mist, the fluid ejects as a **coherent, high-velocity liquid needle** that draws out into a single continuous thick rope in mid-air.

### 2. Coaxial Solution Blow Spinning (The High-Velocity Air Sheath)
- This is the secret to industrial synthetic fiber spinning.
- **Concept:** A central fluid tube surrounded by an outer ring of compressed gas (from a compressed air duster can, bicycle CO2 cartridge, or hand pump).
- **Mechanism:** The high-velocity gas sheath exerts tremendous aerodynamic drag on the fluid core, pulling and stretching the rubber chains to their maximum mechanical draw ratio while instantly sweeping away solvent vapors.
- **Result:** Ultra-rapid flash-off, longer flight range (2–4 meters), and significantly tighter strand alignment.

### 3. The Multi-Hole "Showerhead" Braider
- Instead of a single orifice, use a cluster of 3 to 5 tiny needle holes ($0.3\text{–}0.4\text{ mm}$) spaced 1–2 mm apart.
- **Mechanism:** Multiple independent filaments exit simultaneously. In flight, their turbulent wake causes them to twist and entangle with each other before landing.
- **Result:** A braided multi-strand cable that is exponentially stronger than a single mono-filament.
