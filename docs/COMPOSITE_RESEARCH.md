# High-Performance Polymer Composites & Crosslinkers for Web Fluid

---

## Executive Summary

Polystyrene (thermocol) has been officially retired due to low tensile strength ($\approx 35\text{ MPa}$) and brittle fracture mechanics. 

To achieve exponential tensile strength in `web-fluid-1.2` (Fevibond synthetic rubber base), we have researched **5 Elite Polymer Composite & Crosslinking Candidates**. These candidates dissolve/mix homogeneously in Thinner SP-85 alongside Fevibond and solidify upon spray to form high-tensile, tough, fiction-like web strands.

## Detailed Breakdown of Top Candidates

### 1. Thermoplastic Polyurethane (TPU) — *The Toughness Champion*
- **Chemistry:** TPU combines flexible polyol soft segments with rigid diisocyanate hard segments.
- **Why it outperforms Polystyrene:** Polystyrene is brittle and snaps under strain. TPU has **double the tensile strength of polystyrene** while stretching up to $500\%$ without tearing.
- **Synergy with Fevibond:** Polychloroprene (Fevibond) + TPU blends are industrial standards for high-stress footwear and aerospace adhesives. They form a single-phase homogeneous liquid in thinner, producing ultra-tough, tear-resistant web filaments upon drying.

### 2. Nitrocellulose (NC) — *The High-Speed Rigid Rebar*
- **Chemistry:** Nitrated cellulose polymer extracted from plant fibers.
- **Why it outperforms Polystyrene:** Nitrocellulose is one of the stiffest, hardest natural polymer derivatives known ($\text{Tensile Strength } \ge 80\text{–}100\text{ MPa}$).
- **Synergy with Fevibond:** Highly soluble in SP-85 thinner. When atomized, NC flashes off instantly, locking the rubber strands into a rigid, non-drooping structural web before landing.

### 3. Polyisocyanates (Desmodur / MDI) — *The Chemical Vulcanizer*
- **Chemistry:** Reactive diisocyanates used in two-part industrial adhesives.
- **Mechanism:** Instead of adding a second solid polymer, a small liquid volume of polyisocyanate ($3\text{–}5\%$) is stirred directly into the Fevibond + SP-85 bath.
- **In-Flight Reaction:** Isocyanate groups react directly with the active chlorine atoms on Fevibond's polychloroprene chains, converting liquid thermoplastic rubber into a **3D covalently cross-linked vulcanized thermoset web**. Tensile strength multiplies by $300\%\text{–}500\%$.

### 4. Kevlar (Aramid) Micro-Pulp — *The Fiber-Reinforced Cable*
- **Chemistry:** Micro-fibrillated poly-paraphenylene terephthalamide fibers.
- **Mechanism:** Microscopic Kevlar fibrils are suspended in the fluid. As the mixture passes through the atomizer orifice, fluid shear aligns all Kevlar micro-fibrils along the strand axis.
- **Result:** Transforms the web from pure rubber into a **fiber-reinforced composite rope** capable of supporting heavy loads.
