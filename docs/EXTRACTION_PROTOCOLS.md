# Standard Operating Procedures: PVAc Extraction from Emulsion Glue

This document provides step-by-step bench protocols to obtain anhydrous (water-free) solid Polyvinyl Acetate (PVAc) from commercial white emulsion glue (e.g., Elmer's Glue-All, Gorilla Wood Glue, or generic PVA craft glue).

---

## ⚠️ Critical Chemical Warning: The Water Contamination Factor

Commercial white glue contains **$45\text{–}55\%$ water by weight**. 

In solution blow spinning, **water is your primary failure mode**:
1. **Solubility Collapse:** While pure acetone dissolves PVAc readily, a binary mixture of $\text{Acetone} + \text{Water}$ severely reduces PVAc solvency. At $>10\%$ water content, PVAc forms an insoluble gummy gel that will not spray.
2. **Flash-Off Suppression:** Water evaporates $\sim 18\times$ slower than acetone and requires $4.3\times$ more energy ($\Delta H_{vap}$) to vaporize. Any residual water prevents in-flight solidification, turning web strands into a wet, runny splatter.

> **Golden Rule:** The extracted PVAc must be **$100\%$ anhydrous** before dissolution into the spray solvent.

## Protocol A: Thin-Film Dehydration & Peeling (Recommended SOP)

This is the cleanest, zero-chemical-waste method. It evaporates the water phase naturally, leaving pure coalesced PVAc sheet with no trapped water.

### Equipment & Materials:
- Commercial white glue (Elmer's Glue-All or similar)
- Non-stick spreading substrate: **Silicone baking mat**, smooth **aluminum foil**, or **HDPE plastic lid / sheet**
- Spreader: Plastic putty knife, old gift card, or spatula
- Heat source: Standard household hairdryer (or heat gun on low)
- Scissors or craft blade
- Airtight glass jar (Mason jar or 100 mL reagent bottle) with solvent-resistant seal

### Step-by-Step Procedure:
1. **Substrate Preparation:** Lay a silicone mat or heavy aluminum foil flat on a work surface.
2. **Thin Film Application:**
   - Pour a bead of wet glue across the top of the sheet.
   - Using the putty knife or card, draw the glue down with firm, uniform pressure to create a **thin, semi-transparent wet film (target thickness: $0.2\text{–}0.5\text{ mm}$)**.
   - *Avoid thick puddles:* Thick sections ($>1\text{ mm}$) will skin over on top, trapping water underneath for hours.
3. **Hot Air Desiccation:**
   - Turn the hairdryer to medium heat and high airflow.
   - Keep the nozzle $10\text{–}15\text{ cm}$ away, sweeping continuously back and forth across the film.
   - Watch the optical transition: White emulsion $\rightarrow$ Milky blue $\rightarrow$ **Optically Clear / Transparent**.
   - Total dry time: **3 to 5 minutes** per sheet.
4. **Peeling & Shredding:**
   - Allow the film to cool for 30 seconds (it becomes leathery and less sticky).
   - Peel the transparent PVAc sheet cleanly off the silicone/foil.
   - Using scissors, cut the sheet into **micro-confetti strips ($2\text{ mm} \times 5\text{ mm}$)**. 
   - *Why shredding is vital:* High surface area allows acetone to penetrate and dissolve the polymer in under 5 minutes without forming an insoluble gelatinous clump.
5. **Gravimetric Batching:**
   - Weigh your dry confetti strips on a digital scale (0.1 g accuracy).
   - Store in an airtight jar until ready to dissolve.

## Protocol B: Direct Acetone Extraction (If Hairdryer is Unavailable)

If heat drying cannot be used, direct solvent coagulation can dehydrate PVAc, but must be performed with a **phase-separation protocol** to prevent water contamination.

### The Phenomenon:
When white glue meets acetone, acetone extracts water instantaneously out of the PVAc emulsion droplets. PVAc coagulates into a rubbery white mass. However, that mass traps water in its interior unless sheared into tiny ribbons.

### Step-by-Step Procedure:
1. Measure **50 mL of acetone** into a glass beaker.
2. Load a 10 mL plastic syringe (no needle) with wet white glue.
3. While stirring the acetone vigorously with a glass or metal rod, **extrude the glue in a thin, continuous string directly into the vortex**.
4. The string will immediately curdle into a stringy, fibrous white curd (PVAc phase-separated from water).
5. **The Dehydration Step:** 
   - Lift the curd out immediately with tweezers or tongs.
   - Press the curd firmly between folded paper towels to squeeze out all free water and wet solvent.
   - Submerge the pressed curd into a small secondary bath of **fresh, pure acetone** (10 mL) for 1 minute to strip final residual moisture.
6. The resulting rubbery dough can now be transferred to your primary formulation jar and dissolved in fresh acetone.

## Protocol C: High-Solids Wood Glue Alternative

> **Pro-Tip for Faster Yield:**
> Standard craft glue (Elmer's) is $\sim 30\text{–}35\%$ solids. Commercial **PVA Wood Glue (Titebond I or Gorilla Wood Glue)** is formulated at **$48\text{–}55\%$ solids** and higher molecular weight.
> - Yields nearly double the dry PVAc per unit volume.
> - Dries faster under the hairdryer.
> - Produces higher molecular weight ($M_w$) polymer chains, which dramatically improves strand formation during spraying!
